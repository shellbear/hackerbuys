I used to work in VFX, and for our big bright displays in the office we used digital signage displays. I started using them at home in about 2018, after my nice medium budget sony TV died in a way that I couldn't repair. I currently have an old HD NEC 48" display attached to an apple TV, and a freeview recorder for broadcast (although 98% of TV is done via apple TV) Pros: the screen is bright and adjustable. You can, if you wish fiddle with the colours and brightness The screen is designed for 24/7 duty cycle, so is built like a fucking tank It has all the HDMI you would ever need, plus displayport compute module ports (if that floats your boat) you can also get an ethernet remote control module (never tried it so it might be shit) Second hand, they are dirt cheap. on ebay there are a couple of them in various sizes for > anything else I have used, apart from maybe a humax. Amazon firestick is frustratingly slow/unreliable. They also break after a year or so. Yes the apple TV is probably 5x the price, but the time to playing a show is about 7 seconds, from first pressing the remote to wake everything, to navigating to the show I want. everything else I have used is horribly slow in comparison.

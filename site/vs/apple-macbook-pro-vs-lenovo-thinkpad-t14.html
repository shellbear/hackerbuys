What Linux computer can you buy with the battery life, quietness, lack of heat and speed of a modern ARM based Mac? Battery life, probably none. On the ThinkPad I can run NixOS, which is generally much nicer than macOS. The primary thing that my MacBook has over my ThinkPad are battery life and a bunch of really good Mac applications like the Affinity Suite. But since more and more applications are switching to Electron, it has become less of a problem. Heck, I even have 1Password with fingerprint unlock, etc. like if it was a MacBook. Just got a new T14 (3. gen Intel, 14'') for my personal computing needs, while also using a 16'' Macbook Pro M1 for work. I have to say the T14 is a really nice machine and feels quite modern. So yeah, Lenovo has still some catching up to do but it seems they're going in the right direction again.

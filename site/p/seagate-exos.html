Sorry, but this is amusing to me. ZFS on TrueNAS is probably fine, but you're building your production NAS, to replace the Synology device you've become "so dependent on". I was facing this choice recently, and I agreed with the other tech savy person in the household that we should just use good old LVM + Btrfs.

The M1 Ultra was unable to take full advantage of the excellent RAM speed, but with the M2 and the M3, performance is improving. Just be prepared to spend 5000€ or more for a M3 Ultra. Nvidia Ampere and Apple silicon (AFAIK) are also missing FP4 support in hardware, which doesn't help.

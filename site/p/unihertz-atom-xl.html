I have a unihertz Atom XL. Pros: - Battery lasts for 3 days with my usage (browsing when not at my desk, whatsapp, a handful of calls, android auto) - Rugged / waterproof (IP68) - Fits nicely in your hand - 48 MP camera - not as good as Pixels, but good - Good dual SIM setup Cons: - Thick; probably mostly due to the battery. Doesn't bother me, but if you wear skinny jeans and carry your phone in your front pocket it'll be noticeable - Just got Android 11 - The built in walkie talkie is something of a gimmick since it chews up battery in standby/monitor mode. I thought it would be a useful backup since we live in the sticks w/o reliable phone signal. Get the Atom L instead

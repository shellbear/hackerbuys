Sennbeiser HD600 or HD650 (very similar sound), AKG K7XX, Hifiman HE400. All open. Many other nice open headphones on the market, but those are best bang per buck. For closed, most are not good except the Beyerdynamic DT770 (and the far pricier Fostex TH900). You may consider an external DAC and amplifier if you listen to a laptop — this will help with case noise and power. Beware, however: this is where you get close to snake oil products. Most amps do not make a difference, and a lot depends on your source. An iPhone is an excellent source for most headphones, for example (including all of the above). A reasonable DAC-amp combo is the O2+ODAC, and it is DIY if you can solder. The Schiit stuff in the link above is pretty meh, even though it is often recommended (crappy volume pot in the cheaper units).

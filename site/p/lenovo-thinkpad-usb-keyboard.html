As a mousepad I am using an aluminium/teflon mousepad I have for like 20 years now. It is superflat and doesn't make my arms stick to it while moving. As to your mouse movements: the thinkpad usb keyboard has an additional middle mouse key, which does exactly that. It's just so comfortable to use while browsing. For lots of mouse movements, like in CAD tools or other mouse-heavy environments I still stick to using my vertical mouse though.

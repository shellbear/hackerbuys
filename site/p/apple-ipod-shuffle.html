I hated that the Apple store calls their salespeople "geniuses". I hated fanboys and Steve-Jobs-worship. Steve Jobs was an asshole. It was such a relief to get back to using ThinkPads. Fuck that. I gave it to my kids. They don't use it either. I gradually realized that Apple is not a tech company but a marketing machine for high-margin consumer gadgets that lock users in and squeeze every last dollar out of them.

I upgraded to an Asus Zephyrus G14 (2021 edition) at the end of last year, as my T470s was getting a bit slow. The dimensions are smaller than the Thinkpad, but it weighs slightly more (1.7kg). The thing is though this is a gaming laptop... under the hood it has a Ryzen 9 5900HS (8c/16t), 32GB RAM and a Nvidia RTX 3060. It runs the test suite more than twice as fast as the Thinkpad it replaced :D When using the integrated graphics and with performance set to quiet the fans rarely spin up. It gets around 10 hours battery life. No issues with Linux. My only complaints are that it has issues with USB PD charging and the power brick is a bit big, and there is no webcam (fixed in the next gen version)...

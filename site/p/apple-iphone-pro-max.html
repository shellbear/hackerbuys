Apple phones have always been performance competitive with Android phones for quite a few years now, despite using cooling systems that maintain significantly lower wattage. This means that Apple's processors are more efficient than the equivalent Android devices, but their cooling systems are very much critically designed. Rather than decrease the CPU performance, they typically start dimming the screen first. This is extremely frustrating when outside and you need to see something in the sun, if the sun happens to warm up the screen. This is widely regarded as the biggest problem with Apple phones and thermal management. One user on YT who works on commercial HVAC on a roof in AZ, US mentions that Apple phones are unusable because the ambient temperature and sun will cause the phone to shut down and display a overtemp warning without doing anything, while Samsung keeps working. The reason flagship Android devices do not have this problem is twofold: 1) Android device makers use a ton more cooling materials. Large area graphite film layered with a competition driven (among all Chinese manufacturers anyway) almost 70% coverage vapor chamber cooler. Only Samsung lags the industry here. 2) Different trade offs.

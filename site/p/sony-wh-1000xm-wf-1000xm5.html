Apple plays the 'it's the best for most people' game, not the 'technically ahead in [one or a few feature categories]' game. These products are destined to fail because they make simple things complicated, untrustworthy, bothersome. People are whole-experience buyers, not single-feature buyers, and the experience people want is the magical 'I put it in and it works' experience. What people want is all the upside of the magic of technology and none of the cognitive overhead associated with it. The marketing reflects the inherent desire of the customer.

It was made in 2019, if I could hand it to you I think you'd reconsider your beliefs about what is and isn't possible, it's a very well-made device, the keyboard is lovely. Software support for this is hit-or-miss, but I'm going to miss it when I have to get rid of it for an all-touch device.

So I got into this a bit over the past year, and put a SmartThings socket behind just about everything in the house. Not so much for the cost saving (which will take forever at £29 for each of the SmartThings sockets), but more to understand where the power is being used so that we can run for longer on solar + batteries. There's a range of devices that consume practically nothing when they are in standby mode, these include: - Phone chargers (0 - 1W) - Projectors (0 - 1W) - TVs (0 - 3W for the ones we have) - Laptops (3 - 4W for the ones we have) - AV amps e.g. Denon (2W) - Coffee Machine e.g. Jura (4W) - Printers (0 - 1W) There's a bunch of low powered devices that stay on all the time - Amazon Echo Show (4W) - Presumably the Echos without a screen but I haven't tested them If I turned all of these two groups off in the house when we are not using them then we might save close to the £30/year, but we have a lot of them and it's not worth the effort.

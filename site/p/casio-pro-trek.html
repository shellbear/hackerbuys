The G-Shock square is important because its history and can be had for peanuts, making it a must for a collection. But I argue its not the Casio you want to wear now. It's bulky compared to the display, the buttons are small, the interface antique, the straps are not the most comfortable. For a beater sports watch I wear a Pro-Trek PRW-60Y https://www.casio.com/sg/watches/protrek/product.PRW-60Y-1A/ Tough solar movement, more comfortable, better dial to body ratio, large buttons, better interface, has barometer, altimeter, compass, thermometer, looks better and mature. For a casual watch check out the Casio Oceanus line. Also check out the premium G-Shock models mentioned in the article: MTG and MRG. The MTG in natural metal color is a very mature G-Shock you can wear with almost anything that is not a formal suit. And has a fit and finish that is unbeatable for the price.

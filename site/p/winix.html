I have 4 Coway and 2 Winix purifiers and I can confirm: they're great sleep noise generators! The plastic front pieces and the filters themselves block/baffle any high frequency noise from the fans, leaving you with a pleasant low/mid frequency whooooooosh. I like it better than the sound from small cheap solid state machines; their small speakers can't do low frequencies. For most circumstances where someone would use a white noise machine, this kind of small purifier is all-around better. Hmm. The word "most" is doing a lot of work here. Depends if you enjoy fresh air when you sleep or not. If you've ever monitored CO2 levels you know how quickly they climb in a closed room. But, if you are blowing fresh air into a room, or have the windows open, the filtration effectiveness of these units approaches zero pretty rapidly. And these air purifiers aren't exactly free to run: they use ~50-100W of power and if you run them 24/7 you can expect to spend $100+ per year on filters. So there's no perfect answer...

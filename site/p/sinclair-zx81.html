> I have to imagine that was more intense with the shite keyboard on these things. Yes and no. Yes, because the keys were not great, obviously. And no, because the ZX81 and the later Spectrum had a massively clever optimisation. A big chunk of the lexer and parser was implemented in a way that was kind of coupled to the keyboard driver. So you never typed out GOTO/GOSUB etc., in full. In fact you could not type out a BASIC keyword in full; the keyboard would not let you. At the points where your program could syntactically accept a BASIC keyword, the keys went into keyword mode and would not produce letters at all. So pressing G or H at that point would not get you a letter, but GOTO or GOSUB. This was unusual then and is unheard of now. But it helped make some aspects of coding much more kinaesthetic. And it ended up being quite quick to key in listings, especially if you replaced the keyboard with an aftermarket unit.

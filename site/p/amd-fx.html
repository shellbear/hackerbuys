i used an fx-6300 for years and it was fine. for most use cases the practical need for desktop cpu performance plateaued 10 years ago. during that time adding more memory and moving from hdd to ssd made a much larger difference in user experience than increasing the cpu performance. the biggest problem for amd was not the architecture of the cpu, but the lack of integrated graphics on high end parts, and power efficiency on mobile. both of which were caused by being stuck on an older process. it's also worth remembering that internally amd had a grand vision of unifying the cpu and gpu (HSA) which would have added a bunch of fp compute to the integrated chip, but that vision required a lot of other things to go right, and a lot of them went wrong, so it never happened.

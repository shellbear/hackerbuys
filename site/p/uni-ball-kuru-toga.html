I digitise some of my handwritten stuff, but not all of them, just what I really want to keep or be searchable. For that I created a simple Shortcut on my iPhone: take a photo > rotate it > resize by 20% > save file > rename > share with Obsidian and add it to either Daily Note or a specific note. In Obsidian I have a Claude integration (Claudian Plugin) with an /ocr skill, which runs optical character recognition on the uploaded image and corrects any mistakes and adds some markdown formatting. I have a pretty ugly handwriting but it still does a great job and gets it like 95% right. All this results in a lightweight (~200kb) image in chosen note in my Obsidian vault with a transcribed and edited text below it. Generally I’m a big fan of Obsidian because it’s very intuitive and customisable.

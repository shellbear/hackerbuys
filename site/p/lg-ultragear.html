To go back, you reverse it: move the mouse off the right side of the screen and hit the keyboard shortcut. I'm still more than bitter, after buying some nice 27" 4K HDR 144Hz monitors that Apple actively broke (and may still be broken) Display Stream Compression 1.4 for the Pro Display XDR.

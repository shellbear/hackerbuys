Between that, USB support and Windows support is what made it take off. The iPhone was 2G, overpriced to the point where Apple had to cut prices six months in and didn’t support third party apps. Not having Flash support was really a big deal back then. The first generation Apple Watch along with the apps and the OS were atrocious. Third parties couldn’t make native apps.

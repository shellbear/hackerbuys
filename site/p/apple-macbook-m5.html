You won't do this without some serious local GPU's with big memory. The downside is the speed, it's not fast, but fast enough to tinker and develop with worrying about ongoing cloud cost. My $5k macbook can do more than a $50k nvidia/intel/amd setup, just not as fast. So you need to decide whats important to you if you want to work locally, large/many models or speed. It's the pick 2 problem speed, size, cost, pick 2 or go with cloud and accept your development time is also spent $$ on each iteration.

You wouldn't use resistance heating at -5℉ with a reasonably modern heat pump, such as Mitsubishi or Fujitsu models designed for cold climates. The Mitsubishi ones don't become less efficient than resistive heating until the temperature drops below -15℉. They are 200% efficiency at 0℉ and 300% at 32℉. Their capacity drops from 100% at 23℉ to 76% at -13℉ so you would want to go with a slightly bigger system than you would in a place that doesn't get so cold.

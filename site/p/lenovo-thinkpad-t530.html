I had to do a similar thing with my T530. I wanted to upgrade to a WiFi 6+Bluetooth card. The built in Bluetooth adapter is very old, I think v4.2. It's also a finicky module with a mezzanine connector, and it's nestled deeply into the internal magnesium frame so the range is abysmal. I reflashed the BIOS to kill the whitelist, but I'm left with a bigger problem. The WiFi slot offers PCI, but not USB. The WWAN slot has USB, but not PCI. I ended up tapping into the original Bluetooth connector to steal the USB lines and route them back to the PCI connector. I had some issues at first with the device not enumerating reliably, but it eventually settled down. Now I have WiFi 6 and Bluetooth 5.

I also seem to have brand loyalty to Adidas, but not because of their brand, but rather every time I go shopping for new runners, it’s Adidas shoes that just feel right, with their sock like uppers that accommodate my wide feet perfectly, while also having incredibly light and flexible soles, with a lot of cushioning (because I’m not the lightest runner- so absorbing impact is important for my ankles). I usually expect a pair of running shoes to last me at least 6 months, but ideally a year.

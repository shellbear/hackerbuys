As my work develop is focused on macOS and Windows apps, I need a Windows laptop and got a light Prestige 13 inch with 32GB and 125H. I had some strange steps to like connect/disconnect charger, change MSI Center performance settings. none was reliable. It was amazing how Ubuntu ran so sleek especially comparing to the MacBook Pro 2018 with macOS. - x86_64 feels less portable than arm. Since I got MBP from my work I’ve also got another machine for Windows. I’ve went with 13” MSI Perstige with 125H which was the latest back then offering hybrid cores (performance + economy). TL;DR * Unless you need specific software, Linux distros are great and fast.

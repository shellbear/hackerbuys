I used to have a Kindle Voyage, it was a really neat device. Amazing screen, capacitive buttons to turn pages. The cons it had, and the reason I sought to replace it where the lack of water proofing and the micro USB port. The software was a bit of an issue as well, but with calibre you could reasonably transfer books that weren't from Amazon. Once I hacked the voyage and installed Koreader in it, damn, it completely gave new life to the device. But ultimately the hardware had become lacking, particularly the charging port. In my search for a new reader I bought a Chinese reader (onyx poke 3). Android based, quickly I realized android was not the right operating system for my use-case and the battery sacrifice of the OS was not worth it. Also, the screen just wasn't as good. I read online it's because of some extra layers that are put on modern eReaders to make them more accurate to the touch or something. I think you would never notice unless you had precisely a Kindle Voyage (which had and probably still has the best looking E reader screen). In any case, I ended up buying a Kobo Clara 2E. Waterproof, USB C and (a new requirement for me) compatible with Koreader. Seemed to fit the bill, and it did. The screen is not flushed and there's no buttons which means reading in the bathtub is not the most ideal but still, it's serviceable in this regard. And the screen looks just as good as my Kindle Voyage. So it's pretty close to perfect. Also the screen is not super big which is…

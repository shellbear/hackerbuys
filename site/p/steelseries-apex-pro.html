Because I found just about the perfect keyboard and it's not wireless: Steelseries Apex Pro ( https://www.amazon.com/SteelSeries-Apex-Mechanical-Gaming-Ke... ) With some lube, this Hall effect keyboard comes as close as I've found since the Tom Knight MIT AI lab Microswitch keyboards of yore.

I would like to be able to change the volume with a single key press, or step the debugger, or refresh the webpage, etc. And that's an absolute pain to do if I need to press multiple keys, especially if the keys I'd need to press would be designed for "one fluid movement", basically requiring two hands. What I'm trying to say is that I see a case for actually having both possibilities on the same keyboard. They don't have to be exclusive.

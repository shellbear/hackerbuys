The Quest is a really curious piece of technology. I wouldn't recommend buying one, but see if you can find the opportunity to try one; the VR experience for $300-400 is pretty damn impressive. The differences between the first and second revisions are also quite interesting, especially since the Quest 2 was designed to sell at a lower price point, before inflation drove the price back up. Furthermore, the lens on the Quest 1 is a blisteringly bright 90hz OLED display, whereas the Quest uses a 120hz LCD panel. A really curious side-grade that still has people arguing over which version is better. The software experience is middling (pretty bad if you use any online services whatsoever), but the quality of the hardware for the price is nearly impeccable. Having tried both models, I think I still prefer the muted footprint of the Quest 1, and I'm holding out for a nice replacement OS someday so I can use it as a purely tethered headset.

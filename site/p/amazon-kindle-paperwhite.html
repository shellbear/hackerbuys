nimih, 2026-10-13 in “GPT-6 Astra Helped Me Jailbreak My Kindle”
Doesn’t like it
I have a nice Kindle Paperwhite, but I hardly ever use it. Instead, I read my Kindle books on my S24 Ultra. Why? One is the classic photographer's question: "What's the best camera?" The one you have with you. The other is that I really like scrolling instead of page-turning. I can hold the phone in one hand and use my thumb to scroll up and down just like any webpage or document. This lets me keep my context instead of losing it at every page turn. There are two steps to enable this. First, open any book and tap somewhere in the middle. Select the "Aa" icon at the top. Go to the Layout tab at the top and turn on Continuous Scrolling. Now you will have a problem. The selfie camera's circular hole cuts into the scrolling area. At least to my eyes, it is incredibly disorienting and annoying to have all the rest of the screen scroll but that camera cutout sits there. To fix this, go to Settings / Display / Camera cutout. Scroll down to Kindle and change its setting from "Auto" to "Hide Camera cutout". This takes away a few pixels at the top of the screen, but you have plenty of display space available. And no disorienting black circle as you scroll. You may still prefer a physical Kindle device, but with these two settings, the S24 Ultra is now my favorite Kindle.

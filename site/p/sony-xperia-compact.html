The size and price are similar to those of the former Sony Xperia Compact series. I have used phones from that series for many years, until Sony discontinued them, and I was very content with them. After that, I had to reluctantly use a much bigger phone. The Dimensity 7500 is a good SoC for what is now a mid-range phone (though a few years ago $500 to $600 would have been a flagship phone, not a mid-range phone). Dimensity 7500 has Arm cores of the 2026 generation, implementing the Armv9.3-A ISA, i.e. including the Scalable Matrix Extension, so they will not have any software compatibility problem for many years from now, but the "big" cores are small cores, not much faster than Cortex-A78 or Cortex-A725 (nowadays the cores from the Arm company come in 4 sizes, and a cheap SoC like Dimensity 7500 uses cores of the 3rd size and of the 4th size). Even so, a mid-range phone like this will still be faster than a flagship phone from 6 years ago. In any case the speed will be adequate for anyone who wants a small phone, because they use a laptop for the things that would require a big screen and high speed (and I belong to such people).

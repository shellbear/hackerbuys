Speaking as someone who first used a Mac Plus, graduated to an SE/30, and is now on a Mac mini m4 pro… I can remember when Macintosh was widely held to be an acronym for Most Applications Crash, If Not, The Operating System Hangs. The software has always been terrible, until you try the other guys’ stuff. The hardware has often been good, and is in a purple period right now. Enjoy it while it lasts! (It won’t).

Doesn’t like it
I used to call the Macintosh the user friendly computer that will kill you with a smile since the early models (the 128k, the 512k, and the Plus) had exposed power supplies once the case is open. Staying clear of the CRT is usually not an issue when turning on a machine while testing it, but your hand is within a centimeter of exposed AC mains power. Needless to say I did end up getting shocked a couple of times and the physical sensation is interesting. It really made me appreciate modular designs, where the power supply and CRT are completely fenced off in its own enclosure.

I am a huge fan of M5Stack. Take a look at the Tough or Core units. You get an ESP32 with couple of peripherals (speaker, buzzer, real-time clock) and a TFT touchscreen all in a case. Water-resistant in the case of the M5Stack Tough. I've shipped a couple of consulting products based on them and it really cuts down on the work I need to do in order to build product.

Neutral
I have been using the M5Stack Tough for a product. It has worked reasonably well, though I have had a 10% defect rate on the touch digitizers. I will probably end up switching over to a product from https://www.makerfabs.com/ for a larger display. But it has worked fairly well. The stack was: -Platform.io -ESP-IDF framework -LVGL an embedded ui framework that is pretty simple to use. -FreeRTOS Works great, its been reliable and it will probably remain my choice for the next couple years. One thing I would like is better Rust support. I have taken a look around and everything is just out of date bindings to C/C++ projects. Not a whole lot of native Rust, so I don't really feel the need to switch until that changes.
karmicthreat, 2023-02-04 in “ESP32 Buyer’s Guide: Different Chips, Firmware, Sensors”

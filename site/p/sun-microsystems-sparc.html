I was working in a UK financial blue chip (FTSE 100/high street bank) from 1999 to 2008 where I got my main Sun exposure. I loved the Sun kit and Solaris. Touched everything from Sparc workstations to E10K/E15K. It felt like home! A random selection of areas where they went wrong from my viewpoint as a junior to senior unix admin at the time.. Biggest of all - IBM did a complete number on them in my workplace. Hugely aggressive presence. We had a regular Sun PS person onsite. IBM dropped a whole bunch of people in. Sales, pre sales tech, anything we wanted. Completely undercut Sun and leveraged mainframe deals to push their p5 kit. Before we knew it there was a 'unix consolidation project' and I found myself grumpily using a HMC and bloody AIX. Millions in lost revenue. Went from a tiny IBM presence in a lovely multicultural Unix estate (HP-UX, Solaris, AIX, even Dynix) to bland banks of Power racks in the datacentres. A complete capitulation.

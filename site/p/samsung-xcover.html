Why would they break more often? I don't really see that. We have thousands of Xcovers (also replaceable) in the factories at work and they break no more often than the regular phones in the office environment. And these are not rugged models. And a Fairphone battery is 40€. An Xcover battery (including NFC antenna which is weirdly enough in the battery) costs similar. The screen 90€.

Problem #1: Never mind the Metaverse, I bought an Oculus Quest and all I can say is one of Steve Jobs signature terms: "It's brain dead." Imagine you excitedly get your glasses charged up and turned on and then you are confronted with connecting to the wireless network, when you don't know how to work the controllers and finding the wireless setup is disorienting, especially since it's not clear what the buttons do so you don't even know how to select anything. It's ridiculous that this is your first very frustrating experience with VR. Solution: Put a small panel on the outside like most printers have for connecting to the wireless or allow controlling the headset from a PC so you can use your normal mouse and keyboard (a phone would work too) to setup the device Problem #2: My unit won't update.

Yeah I bought a Panasonic plasma TV in 2006 when I got married and at the time LCD was in its infancy, very jerky and barely watchable. The only advantage of LCD at the time was that plasma was not available in sizes below 38" and was slightly more expensive. Furniture has changed too since then; with HD it's unlikely that you'll be anything below 45" these days. I still have it in fact, I don't like how power hungry it is but it doesn't really matter for the very little time that I watch TV (which is mostly during the day in the weekend, when my electricity is self produced solar anyway). That said it's probably time to get a new OLED TV and I am just waiting for the day that I will need new furniture.

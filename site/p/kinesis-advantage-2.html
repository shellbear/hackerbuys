I have been using the Kinesis Advantage 2 with brown switched for 5+ years. The biggest advantage is its ergonomic design. It's a fixed split with vertical keys and good palm support. It took me two weeks to get used to it even though I was doing touch typing for a long time before that, and it took me a month to get back to my typing speed. I've done some remapping to make it more comfortable for coding. It's definitely not an ideal keyboard for gaming unless you make a dedicated layout for gaming.

I have six drones, an Osmo Pocket 2, Insta360 Go 2, GoPro, etc but I barely use the pocket cameras because the workflow to extract content for day-after story-telling via phone feels quite tedious. If you were only going to ingest footage post-trip and make a piece (as per the YouTube example), then I think it's less painful. A decent phone with good stabilisation can handle that though. That said, two advantages for the Osmo Pocket: - footage is not clogging up your phone storage, which can be particularly annoying if you are often unable to backup to the cloud - it is literally pocket-sized, a nice form-factor compared to GoPro, and pretty quick to get out and use.

I moved from an original Canon 5d to the X100F a few years back. The immediate improvement in AF was jarring. So was the output straight from the camera. Then there was the incredible size- I could just slip it into my jacket pocket. I moved up the the X100V when that came out to try and get even better AF performance (most pictures are of my busy kids) and it has been a worthy upgrade. Since then I’ve tried an X-T3 and an XPro2. I fairly quickly sold both. The XT3 is just too big for me to want to take out. The XPro2 is smaller but still much bigger than the X100. As hard as I tried to justify spending more money, the X100v is basically perfect for my needs. A Leica Q2 is on my wishlist but I wonder if it too would feel too big to take with me most of the time. It’s true that the best camera is the one you have with you and because of the size of the X100, you can basically have it with you all the time.

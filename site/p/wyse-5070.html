Get a Wyse 5070 or anything like that instead of a raspberry pi as a stable small server. Used they are basically the same price point. I have 3 running. One got a RAM and SSD upgrade and serves as my desktop/media thingy/TV and mobile audio lap in my camper.

Likes it
I recently switched from a Pi4 to a Wyse 5070 ($35 second hand off eBay if you're patient). Apart from HomeAssistant, it's running all of my local services. It's significantly faster. It's also running from an SSD instead of SD, this is a big deal because SDs are known to die pretty quickly. The undisclosed hero feature is QuickSync. Jellyfin was practically unusable on the Pi4. The only thing I'm missing is somewhere to slot in an Arc GPU. I'm pretty certain that some light local LLM inference would be possible.
zamalek, 2024-04-01 in “What someone learnt when they replaced their Pi 5 with a Amazon mini desktop”

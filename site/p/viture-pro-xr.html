I like glasses like these, but just for a simple use case. I picked up a pair of factory refurb VITURE Pro XR for $130 and they are fantastic for watching movies from my phone on a trip, or playing games from like a steam deck. They really are native 1080p 120Hz micro OLED screens. Fantastic contrast and brightness. I don't care at all about the head tracking or passthrough or any of that stuff. to me they are just a simple fixed floating huge high quality screen that I can use on the go. I'm also able to watch 3D movies on them too with of course zero crosstalk and that's pretty neat too. If anyone else is interested in trying these types of glasses here they are for $152 (with coupon SHOPNOW15) certified refurb from the company with an excellent 30 day return policy. https://www.ebay.com/itm/389249036434

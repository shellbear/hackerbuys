I like the idea of being able to use this with classic miniature based war games such as Warhammer where the cost of the physical figurines alone could easily reach the price of this headset. However I see two issues there, number one is pricing. It's unclear to me how much the games themselves would cost and this headset is already relatively expensive particularly since it's marketed as a group activity - just three players already runs you $1000. That's a lot of money to drop on a VR related device that has significantly more limited applications as opposed to a more generalized system such as the upcoming Apple vision, and the oculus. And number two is that you have to use a wand to manipulate the board and the pieces, and I question how accurate this will be if there are literally hundreds of pieces on the board. I could see it becoming extremely frustrating very quickly. And I'll be honest, and this is not the fault of tiltfive but I really like the weight of a pewter figurine in my hand and the lack of any haptic feedback might bother me.

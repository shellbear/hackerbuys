If you’re dead-set on buying online, look for a local boutique somewhere and see if they sell online as well. They generally have good return policies, and in my experience boutiques tend to have much higher quality pieces—generally orders of magnitude more longevity than some of my H&M or Uniqlo stuff, for example.

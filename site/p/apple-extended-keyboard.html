They didn't leave the functions out. They left the words off the keys and put the functions on Command-ZXCV, which you only know if someone already told you. The Extended Keyboard later printed Cut/Copy/Paste under F2-F4, or shipped a plastic overlay. That is a confession, not a design. Xerox Star put labeled keys in a left-hand cluster meant to work with the mouse in the other hand, which is the Engelbart pairing. Apple chose not to. Printing Copy on C would have been the cheap half of that lesson. The missing keycaps are still the small complaint. The theft was the words.

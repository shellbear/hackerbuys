Likes it
Also got a Breville microwave, too the "combi-wave" one or something. Amazingly quiet. I usually hate all device sounds, but surprisingly this has a reasonably quiet singular bell-like "ding" option, rather than the typical "beep-beep-beep" that drives me nuts. The only thing that concerns me is the oven modes. It's got this plastic spinner piece that supports the glass turntable that I'm not sure about leaving in, and you can't use the metal tray in microwave mode. So I just leave it in microwave mode all the time.

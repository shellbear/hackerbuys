Not mentioned under robots is Makeblock [0], which I've had a lot of fun with with my 4-year-old. It was simple enough to put together with him, but also complicated enough that he still looks back at that as an achievement. There's an app with a remote control which he's loved and has helped him get familiar with the way the robot moves, but it's also programmable with a fork of Scratch (with blocks for the specific sensors and motors on the robot) and Arduino C. Scratch is still a bit over my son's head, but he's enjoyed figuring out which blocks fit where, and I can usually help him tweak his program to produce a visible result, much to his delight. Most days I'll also build something more complex while he looks over my shoulder telling me what he wants it to do. [0] https://www.makeblock.com/pages/mbot-robot-kit

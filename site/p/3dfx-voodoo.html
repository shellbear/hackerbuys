Maybe you're too young, but in the late 90's 3Dfx was so hot. They weren't the first GPU, but they were the first to break out and make it big in the PC space. Then the GeForce 2 came along and it was clearly better than any 3Dfx offering, while people were tired of waiting for a new Voodoo card. The rest is history.

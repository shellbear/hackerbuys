For the smaller USB-native flash drives, I have not noticed any particular pattern to their unreliability, but I trust them even less. For NVMe-based portable SSDs in particular, I am wary in part because of the long-running thread on the AnandTech forums documenting how all three suppliers of NVMe to USB bridge chips (JMicron, Realtek, ASMedia) have had extensive problems with both reliability and compatibility. Companies like Samsung and SanDisk can certainly be expected to do more QA on their products incorporating those chips than smaller brands, but that's not a guarantee that they'll avoid the problems from their upstream supplier.

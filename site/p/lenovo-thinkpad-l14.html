I recently got two; a refurbished HP EliteBook 645 G10 and a used Lenovo ThinkPad L14 Gen 2. Both around $300, with a recentish processor (Zen 3), 16 GB ram and a 256 GB SSD. Scratches and dings included at no additional fee. The Thinkpad has fewer USB ports, but the Elitebook has an annoying fan. If you need more than 16 GB ram, you probably need to consider if it's better to get one that already has that much, or if you should get one with replaceable RAM and swap... 16 GB is enough for my needs; one of the machines has 1 channel soldered, 1 DIMM; the other machine is 2 DIMMs. I was doing the same work on a $100 laptop with 4 GB ram; it wasn't fun, but I could do it --- but if my needs change I can upgrade. The "refurbished" machine had a proper out of the box experience and windows 11 professional; the used one had win 11 enterprise installed with a user named user --- I put a fresh copy of windows 11 pro on that (bios activation); after some judicious changes in group policy editor, and disabling the push content in the lock screen, it's not terrible.

I just bought a Huawei matebook x, and I'm blown away by it. I'm running Debian with gnome on Wayland. I get about 10 hours of battery life, and suspend/resume seems to work flawlessly. USB-c is awesome to have, and it comes with a dock dongle for legacy ports. The bad: * Fingerprint reader doesn't seem to work. * Audio chipset is weird and only one speaker outputs sound (headphones work as expected). * Function layer keys don't work (volume/brightness adjustment)

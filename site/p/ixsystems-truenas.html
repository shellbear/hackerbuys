Exactly. I searched a lot for a small form factor ZFS backup server. The vast majority of mini PCs don’t expose a PCIe or eSATA expansion slot. You can connect HDDs with USB, but USB connection is not preferred with ZFS. The mini PCs can contain dual SSD, but, as noted, the storage is limited and the price is high. The closest options I found are: RockPro64, Odroid HC4, AsRock Deskmini X300, ZimaBoard, RPI CM4, each having own problems. One may build one from an mITX motherboard, but frankly that may also cost about a TrueNAS mini (which is quite large). Synology boxes are nice, but I want ZFS! If someone knows a small low power system for a ZFS backup server (like a passively cooled SBC or PC with sata port that can be used to connect HDDs placed outside box due to size limitation, or at least something that is portable), including a DIY build, I would love to take a look.

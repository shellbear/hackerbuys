It takes a LONG time, depending on the machine(s) and architectures. pkgsrc has quarterly releases (2022Q1, 2022Q2, and so on). Alpha and UltraSPARC are fast and finish in a month or two, and can build any updates after the first run. SPARC (running on an UltraSPARC Sun Fire V100) and mips64eb (running on an EdgeRouter Lite) finish before the end of the quarter now and then, but I don't count on it. earm and earmv4 may get to the point where they can finish before the end of a quarter because it's trivial to add additional machines, but I haven't had the time or resources to do that yet. Also, there's an issue with distcc and earmv4 I haven't figured out yet. mipsel (Cobalt) and sh3el (Landisk) are not slowpokes, but are run as best effort. The Cobalt is a 250 MHz Cobalt RaQ2 with 256 megs of memory and a 500 gig SATA drive, and the SuperH machine is a 266 MHz SH4 with only 64 megs of memory and a 160 gig IDE disk.

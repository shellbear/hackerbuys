I use the standard hardware encryption of my SSD (a Samsung 830 in my 2012-era Dell XPS 13), which requires I enter the passphrase when I turn my machine on. Advantages: - OS neutral - Seemingly as fast as running 'unencrypted' (I assume performance is identical, the only difference being the passphrase is stored in my head rather than the BIOS) Disadvantages: - Limited to an 8-char (!) ASCII passphrase - I've no idea how secure it really is - Can't audit the algorithm (not that I have the technical ability to)
de_dave, 2016-05-24 in “Ask HN: How do you encrypt your laptops?”

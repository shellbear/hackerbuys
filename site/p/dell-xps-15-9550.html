I bought a refurbed Dell XPS 15 9550 a little over a year ago. I love it. I run Ubuntu on it and I've yet to have any problems. Only negatives are that the webcams in an odd place, the speakers are awful, and it's a bit heavy. The only XPS competitor I considered was various comparable Thinkpad models. Older but still reliable Thinkpads can be incredibly inexpensive, and are also very easy to modify.

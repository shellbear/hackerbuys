I upgraded my home network from a Cisco 3750G to a Brocade ICX 6610 a couple years ago... - 40 GbE to the "new" (in process of being set up and deployed) NAS - 10 GbE to the "old" (being phased out) NAS (was: 40 GbE) - 40 GbE to my workstation - 10 GbE to the "other machine" on my desk - n x 10 GbE to the cluster of ESXi servers - 10 GbE to other misc. servers in the rack - Multiple 10 GbE and 1 GbE links to the PTP GrandMasters and NTP servers - Plus all the various PoE devices (APs, VoIP phones, etc.) and miscellaneous other hosts (NTP appliances, DRACs, PDUs, NetBotz, etc.) at 1 GbE and/or 100 Mbps Unfortunately, I'm just about out of 10 GbE ports so I may need to get another ... Highly recommend the ICX 6610 in particular. The only other Brocade (Ethernet) switches I've personally managed are the "access layer" FCX switches (in an old position 10+ years ago).

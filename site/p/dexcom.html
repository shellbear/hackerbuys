Wearing a Libre 3 sensor from Abbott, I can say I'm happy I don't need the official app. The sensors are good. The app however forces you to be always online and send your measurements to Abbott's servers, if you want the measurements to be available to other apps or devices. It means that if you lose connectivity, or Abbott loses their servers, no integration will work anymore.

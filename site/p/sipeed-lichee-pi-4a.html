Neutral
I got 7.64 SPECint2006 on my Lichee Pi 4A @ 1.85 GHz (same processor, different board). Raspberry Pi 4B @ 1.5 GHz gets 9.22 with the same compiler version and flags. In fairness, the Cortex-A72 in the Raspberry Pi is the nth iteration of Arm cores whereas the C910 is essentially the 1st generation (the C906 is completely different and in-order). I wouldn't make too many conclusions based on this about the future of RISC-V and its implementations, but I'm looking forward to the next wave of higher performing cores coming to market.

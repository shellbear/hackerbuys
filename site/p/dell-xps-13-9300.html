I've had 2 Dell XPS's running Ubuntu, a 9300 and 9370. I very recently switched to an Air M2. The build quality between the XPS and the air is night and day. The 9300 flexed so much that when I picked it up with a single hand, the trackpad would click from the flex. After a couple years, the keyboard stopped working, the shell started to physically split and I had to tighten the case screws every week or so (there was a ~2-3mm gap in the case, to a point where the USBc wouldn't line up). My camera started to turn off at the slightest vibration/breeze. I find OSX painful. But between a mildly frustrating OS and crap hardware...for me, the air is an obvious better buy.

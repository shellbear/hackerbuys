I'd recommend: - Chiclet Keyboard - Inexpensive - Wired - Full QUERTY layout (inc. number pad). - Maybe a dedicated calculator button. - No LEDs/special features/special software. Advantage including: - Lower compression depth/lower pressure per key press. - Lower noise. - Lower cost. - Increased reliability. - Plug and play. Amazon Basics and Dell both make good keyboards in this category but Amazon Basics' keyboard no longer has the dedicated Calculator button. I buy these keyboards on purpose and legitimately don't understand people who WANT a Model-M clone, I typed on those for years, terrible keyboards. Just loud, attract grime, and cause hand fatigue. PS - Although the trendy tide could be turning with MX LOW PROFILE gaining some interest. edit: Maybe check out the CHERRY Stream Keyboard for $30 on Amazon (US).

Not too surprising, a Bambulabs P1P and X1C competitor. The K1 is equivalent to a P1P and K1 Max to a X1C. Pricing is very comparable too (K1 is a bit cheaper). Features are almost exactly the same but Creality isn’t the most famous for customer service and software support. I own two of their printers, one from Ender series and one CR series. Both work fine after some work on it. Would be interesting to see if the K1 is truly print out of the box.

Neutral
I experienced this when I bought a niche 3D printer (a CR30, which is a belt printer). The machine required a lot of finessing to get working properly, and almost all of the information was in discord channels. The people writing and sharing info in those channels were of course very gracious and helpful, but it was frustrating to try and search for things there instead of on a traditional forum.

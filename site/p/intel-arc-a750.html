Meanwhile in AI/ML the industry consolidated around CUDA because OpenCL was never that great, and — Nvidia owning the standard, and having built around it for over a decade, gives them a really big advantage. Plus, every GPU sold in the last decade+ by Nvidia supports CUDA, whereas only the new server GPUs from AMD support ROCm. Nvidia has executed incredibly well for decades: they pioneered shader programming, CUDA, raytracing, and DLSS.
